Kingsfold Crowns

The fair has come to Kingsfold, and so have the horses. You arrive with 100 crowns in your purse.

Study each race card: form, ratings, the going, the trip, who rides and who trains. Tap a horse to see its full record, then back it to win, to place (first three) or each way (both).

The book offers American odds on every runner: +450 means a 100 stake wins 450; -150 means you stake 150 to win 100. Bet the winner, a top-3 finish, head-to-head matchups, the exacta or race specials, and tie picks together in a Same Race Parlay. Prices move as money comes in, and you keep the odds you take.

Read the pace map, the draw, the going, the trip and the form. Then just watch them run. You can still cash out or bet in-running from the Bets button.

Beat Lady Mortimer, the rival gambler, buy and train your own horse, and climb from Peasant punter to King of Kingsfold.

Start a new season? Your purse, the horses and all their form will be replaced.
